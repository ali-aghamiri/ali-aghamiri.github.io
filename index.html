01 / About

Research at the intersection of systems and networks.

I am pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering (Computer Architecture) at the University of Tehran, advised by Prof. Ahmad Khonsari. I study how to make distributed systems more efficient and responsive—from scheduling multi-job distributed training to transaction confirmation in DAG-based ledgers. I have also worked on low-latency video streaming and quality of experience.
