I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Engineering (Computer Architecture) at the University of Tehran, advised by Prof. Ahmad Khonsari. My research interests include distributed systems, networked machine learning, resource scheduling, and performance optimization.

My current work studies how shared compute, storage, and network resources affect distributed training. My M.Sc. thesis investigates tip selection and transaction confirmation in DAG-based ledgers. I have also collaborated on low-latency streaming and quality-of-experience research.

Seeking PhD opportunities. I welcome conversations about research in distributed, networked, and ML systems. Get in touch.

Research Experience

Distributed Machine Learning Systems

2025 – present

University of Tehran · with Dr. Mahdi Dolati

Developed and implemented optimization and heuristic components of NEXUSTRAIN, a multi-job distributed training scheduler that jointly considers hybrid data/pipeline parallelism, GPU placement, compute scheduling, remote-data prefetching, and shared network/storage constraints. Evaluated the framework through numerical experiments and packet-level simulation; a manuscript has been submitted to IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing.

DAG-Based Blockchain Systems

2023 – present

University of Tehran · advised by Prof. Ahmad Khonsari

Investigating tip selection and transaction prioritization for confirmation speed in IOTA’s DAG-based ledger. This includes my M.Sc. thesis and a CSICC 2025 paper.

Networked Video Systems

2023 – 2026

Research collaborations · University of Klagenfurt / ATHENA and IMDEA Networks

Worked on low-latency video streaming, L4S experiments, and quality-of-experience questions for volumetric video.

PhD research direction: I aim to extend this work toward online scheduling for changing job workloads, faster resource-aware heuristics based on a simpler model, and end-to-end evaluation with real distributed-training workloads on GPU systems.
